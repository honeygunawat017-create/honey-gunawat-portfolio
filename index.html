School leadership · Head Girl

A more orderly end to the school day

At dismissal, students from three school buildings crowded together to reach their buses and vans. I discussed the problem with my council members, then we brought a staggered dismissal idea to our coordinator. After the coordinator tried it and we saw it was working, we presented it to the principal, who approved it.

A bell now rings every 10 minutes to dismiss one floor at a time from each building. The system reduced crowding and is still used by the school. The principal thanked me personally.
